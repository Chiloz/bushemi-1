A five-minute demo script

Start with both phones above (or open each app in its own browser tab).

  1. Patient: tap “Continue as Mutinta (demo)”, choose Nurse home visit, open Grace Mulenga, pick a time, pay with simulated MTN Mobile Money.
  2. Professional: choose “Grace Mulenga”. The new request is waiting with a countdown. Notice the patient's details are limited until you accept.
  3. Professional: accept, then set off, arrive, end the visit and write the report. The patient app shows each step live.
  4. Patient: read the visit report, leave a review. Professional: check Earnings and request a simulated payout.
  5. Managed path: book “BUSHEMI Care” in the patient app. After a few seconds the simulated care team assigns Natasha Banda, visible in her app under Assignments.
  6. Join: in the professional app choose “Join BUSHEMI”, submit, see “in review”, then use Demo tools → “Approve my verification” and watch yourself appear in patient search.

One booking, one state machine

Both models use the same record. Independent: requested → confirmed → en route → in progress → report due → completed. Managed starts at “assigning”, handled by the care team.

Built for Zambia

Mobile money (MTN, Airtel, Zamtel), SMS and WhatsApp reminders, landmarks in addresses, voice-call fallback, low-data friendly: no libraries, no web fonts, no build step.

Privacy by state

Professionals see only first name, age and area until they accept. Health summaries are shared only with consent. Unverified professionals are never shown to patients.

Honest about what is simulated. OTP codes appear on screen, payments and payouts are simulated, documents are not uploaded, routes and “live” tracking are illustrative, older reviews are demo content, and all prices and licence numbers are placeholders. Data lives only in your browser.
